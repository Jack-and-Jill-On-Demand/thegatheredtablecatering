Our Story

A table worth gathering around

The Gathered Table grew out of a simple belief: the best events are the ones where the host gets to be a guest. So we took the hospitality, reliability, and care that Jack & Jill On Demand is known for and brought it to the table.

From a styled charcuterie board for a small celebration to a full chafing-dish spread with a serving team, we craft menus around your event, set everything up beautifully, keep it running all night, and clean it all up when the last plate is cleared.

Family owned, locally rooted, and proud to be part of Oregon and Washington's award-winning Jack & Jill family.
